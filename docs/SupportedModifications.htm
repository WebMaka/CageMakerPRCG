Supported Modifications

As of the current release (0.7), CageMaker PRCG supports the following modifications to faceplates and optionally cage backs:

  • Keystone connectors in lock-tab-up or lock-tab-down orientation.
  • Neutrik D-Series connectors, with both two- and four-screw configurations supported.
  • new for version 0.7 :: 85mm x 58mm Single-Board Computers (SBCs) in the Raspberry Pi form factor, in horizontal and two vertical orientations.
  • new for version 0.7 :: Short and tall height cable combs in narrow- and wide-runway configurations.
  • new for version 0.7 :: ATX-standard 159mm x 44.7mm rear-panel IO shield cutout.
  • new for version 0.7 :: PC expansion slot (PCI) in both standard and low-profile heights.
  • Cooling fans in 30 mm, 40 mm, 60 mm, 80 mm, 92 mm, 120 mm, and 140 mm sizes.
  • Ready-to-use round holes for pushbuttons, indicator lights, etc. in 10 mm, 12 mm, 16 mm, 19 mm, and 24 mm sizes.
  • VESA-FDMI mounting bolt patterns, in MIS-B/C/D/E/F sizes.
  • Fractional-DIN cutouts in 1/32-DIN, 1/16-DIN, 1/8-DIN, and 1/4-DIN sizes. Rectangular cutouts are available in both horizontal and vertical orientations.
  • IEC-60309 industrial power inlets in both 16A and 32A capacities, with both three-wire and 4-7-wire sizes supported.
  • IEC-60320 C13/C14/C19/C20 power sockets/outlets, in both screw-mount and snap-in forms, and in both horizontal and vertical orientations.
  • Three user-defined custom cutouts, which can be round or rectangular/square with rounded corners as an option. These cutouts can also be set to include a recessed channel for snap-in panel-mount connectors.
